# Product image prompts

These fictional sample assets were generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool on 1 November 2026. They are AI-generated product mockups, not photographs of inventory or existing customer products. No external source photograph was used. The generated originals were copied into the repository; no image-editing script or API-key fallback was used.

All three outputs were visually inspected for complete products, believable textures and lighting, clean framing, and absence of recognizable manufacturer marks or watermarks. The fragrance label reads “No. 04”.

## headphones-photo.png

Saved project path: `public/samples/headphones-photo.png`

```text
Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: square ecommerce product photograph for a polished independent-seller creative studio. Create a convincing premium studio photograph of a fictional pair of matte graphite over-ear headphones with an adjustable cushioned headband and padded ear cups, shown upright at a subtle three-quarter angle on a low warm-gray stone plinth. Warm ivory seamless backdrop, restrained soft natural side lighting, believable material grain and seams, careful product photography with realistic diffuse reflections and a gentle grounded shadow. The entire headphones including both ear cups and complete headband must be visible, occupy about 65% of the square frame, with generous quiet space around it. Clean editorial catalog style, natural photography, not an illustration, vector, icon, stylized render or CGI. No recognizable real manufacturer design, no brand logo, no words, no captions, no watermark, no humans, no extra props. Palette: graphite, warm light gray, ivory.
```
